Privacy policy

How CamCourse handles your data

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect your data when you use CamCourse services.

1. Data we collect

We may collect account details, learning activity, progress data, and support messages that you provide to us.

2. How we use your data

Your data helps us personalize recommendations, improve course quality, provide support, and maintain platform security.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with trusted service providers required to run the platform.

4. Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting our support team.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at camcourses@gmail.com.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and personalize content. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser.

7. Data retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including legal or reporting requirements.

8. Children's privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers outside of your jurisdiction where data protection laws may differ.

10. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

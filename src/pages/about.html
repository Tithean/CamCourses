Our Story

How it started

CamCourse was founded on the belief that quality technology education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background. We recognized a gap between what students learn in traditional settings and what the tech industry actually needs. Our journey began with a simple mission: to create a practical, modern learning platform that bridges this gap and empowers Cambodian learners to pursue rewarding careers in technology.

Today, we work with universities and industry mentors to design courses that reflect real-world demands. We believe in learning by doing—every course includes projects, challenges, and mentorship to build genuine confidence and job-ready skills.
